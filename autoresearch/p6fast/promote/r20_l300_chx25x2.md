## Rationale (one line each)
- Local 1 kb multinomial on both tracks: initiation sites compete for scanning ribosomes and
  elongating ribosomes share the local ORF, so both tracks are compositional within a region.
- Conv9x2 over the embedding: the start codon, its Kozak-scale context and the P-site frame are read
  from neighbouring nucleotides.
- Factorised CHX: elongation density is a conserved total times a positional distribution.
- Wide CHX trunk: the elongation reading frame is a 3-nt periodicity integrated over tens of codons;
  initiation is local, so the two tracks want different receptive fields.

## Mapping to the slow tier (`src/tisiago/seq2func/model.py`, `train.py`)
- Exists: local loss on both tracks: `--local-weight 300 --local-libraries tis,chx --local-block 1024`
  (also run `--local-weight 100`: the slow tier's Poisson is on genomic-window totals, not per
  transcript, so the relative weight differs). Factorised TIS (`local_softmax`) as in `rnacond_f*`.
- Missing (new head kind, e.g. `rnacond_fcc`):
  - TIS trunk `Conv1D(256, 9) -> relu -> Conv1D(256, 9) -> relu` replacing `Linear(256)` in
    `RnaCondFactTrackHead`; one-sided RNA features kept, CHX contrast removed.
  - CHX on its own trunk `Conv1D(256, 25) x2` with a factorised output (softplus amount + logits,
    same `local_softmax` as TIS) instead of `softplus(Linear(2))`.
- Training: the fast loop needed 30k steps on a frozen backbone; the slow tier's 30k-step fine-tune
  is already in that range.
